HOW TO PLAY

Train your memory

  • 🃏
    Flip two cards to find a pair. Every stage adds one more pair.
  • 🔥
    Chain matches to build a streak. Every 3 in a row earns bonus SP.
  • 👣
    Checkpoints come every 3rd stage. Limited-moves checkpoints give you pairs + 7 moves, growing by 2 each time.
  • ⏱
    Timed checkpoints give unlimited moves, but you must clear the deck before the countdown ends.
  • ✨
    Hints reveal a matching pair briefly (max 2 per level). They cost 5 SP each.
  • 🛒
    Spend SP in the Market on power-ups: Hint ✨, Peek 👁, Time Freeze ❄️, Shield 🛡️ and Extra Moves ➕ (limited-moves checkpoints only). Use them from the bar at the bottom of the game.
